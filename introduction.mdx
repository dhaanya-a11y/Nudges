## Overview

**Nudges** are customer communications designed to encourage customers to take the **next action toward a defined goal**.

A nudge can be used to communicate an action, reminder, or benefit to a customer based on the configured nudge criteria. The communication may be presented as an **in-app experience**, such as a PWA banner, or delivered through an **external communication channel** such as SMS, WhatsApp, RCS, or Email.

The nudge configuration brings together the customer eligibility, communication content, delivery method, and trigger required to send the communication.

<Info>
A Nudge is defined through four key components: **Eligibility, What to Send, How to Send, and When to Send**.
</Info>

### Eligibility

**Eligibility** defines the customers to whom the nudge applies. Customer parameters and other configured criteria determine which customers are considered for the nudge.

### What to Send

**What to Send** defines the communication that is presented or delivered to the customer. The communication uses a configured **template**.

For example:

> **"Hi, complete the goal to unlock your reward."**

### How to Send

**How to Send** defines the channel through which the nudge reaches the customer.

Supported communication experiences include:

- **PWA** — Displays the nudge as an in-app experience, such as a banner.
- **SMS** — Sends the configured nudge as a text message.
- **WhatsApp** — Sends the configured nudge through WhatsApp.
- **RCS** — Sends the configured nudge through RCS.
- **Email** — Sends the configured nudge through Email.

### When to Send

**When to Send** defines the trigger for the nudge. The trigger determines when the configured communication is sent.

For example, a nudge may be configured to be sent at **5:00 PM on October 20, 2026**.

## Nudge Types

Nudges are available in the following types:

| **Nudge Type** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| **System Nudge** | Uses system-based triggers and customer parameters, such as card details, card status, and date of birth. |
| **File Upload** | Uses a `.csv` file upload to trigger nudges. |
| **Journey Nudge** | Sends a nudge through **SMS, WhatsApp, or Email** with a link that directs the customer to a **landing page**, where information from the previous journey step is retained and the customer can proceed to the **next step**. |
| **Promotional Nudge** | Uses datapoints across offers to trigger a nudge. |
| **Cross Sell / Up Sell** | Uses EMI-related attributes to create a nudge. |
| **Other** | Delivers value through gift vouchers. |

<Note>
The configuration and options available for a nudge depend on the selected **Nudge Type**.
</Note>
